2026 Nebraska Medicine & UNMC Staff Virtual Art Exhibition
Healing Arts Gallery · UNMC · Nebraska Medicine · iEXCEL

The 2026 Staff Virtual Art Exhibition displays the talent, originality and creativity of Nebraska Medicine and UNMC employees. The exhibition brings together artists with all levels of experience from various styles, mediums and backgrounds and showcases their original artwork. The Healing Arts Gallery celebrates the intersection of medicine and creativity at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. Open to all Nebraska Medicine and UNMC employees, the 2026 Staff Virtual Art Exhibition presents 73 works by UNMC and Nebraska Medicine employees.

Works marked On view at BCC are included in the physical art exhibition at BCC
